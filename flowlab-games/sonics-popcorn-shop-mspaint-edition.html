Sonic's Popcorn Shop - MS Paint Edition

Release Date: 7/13/2024

A port of SegaSonic Popcorn Shop, with redrawn graphics and English voicelines! Help Sonic and Tails make their popcorn without Eggman's interference!

Controls: Z key to run/push/select, the X and C keys to select, and the enter key to start game. (You may also use the S or L keys to play in full screen.)

Credits: TheGamer2000 (Rendition Lead, Programmer, Tester, Sound Recreation), Dani (Tester), Zachman Awesomeness (Sonic's Voice), MattM4nia (Then SonicFan214, Eggman's Voice), Sega and Sonic Team (Original Game & Source Assets)
