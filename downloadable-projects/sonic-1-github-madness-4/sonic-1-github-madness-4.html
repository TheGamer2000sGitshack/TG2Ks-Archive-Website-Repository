SONIC 1 GITHUB MADNESS 4: WRATH OF TEETH

Lead/Developer(s): DeltaW, Katsushimi, PikaNoob, Sonic ROM Hacking Community

Project Description

The fourth entry in the GitHub Madness series. Spiral into an even greater bout of insanity as you guide Teeth Tonic and Maniac Mouse through the trippy and toothy world!

A two (technically six) month long monstrosity created by Sonic ROM hacking community members with various levels of expertise.

You must have a Sega Genesis/Mega Drive console or emulator to play this project.

This project is not suitable for those under 17 years of age.
