SONIC: NIGHTMARE - A SONIC.ERR RETAKE

Lead/Developer(s): MadnessPrime, Sonic Nightmare Development Team, Kalviniscool123 (Clickteam Version)

Project Description

Many years ago, SEGA developed an AI named "Prime" in an attempt to create an advanced Sonic game. Being fed early concept designs, Prime began to create levels and routes, testing them as it went...

Prime eventually adapted to the game world and claimed it as its own. Years after Sega's abandonment of the project, players got a hold of the game, only to find the game seeming to have a mind of its own and very unsettling.

The AI went rogue, no longer caring for the task it was made for. No matter what the players did, Prime will always find a way to mess with them...

----------------------------------

Play as early versions of Sonic characters as you enter the world of Prime...

This project is compatible on both Sega Genesis/Mega Drive hardware/emulators and on modern Windows machines (via. the Clickteam version).

Note that the Clickteam version is official albeit non-canon to the project's story.

This project is not suitable for those under 15 years of age.
