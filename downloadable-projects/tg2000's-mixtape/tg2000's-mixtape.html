TG2000'S MIXTAPE

Lead/Developer(s): MDTravis, TheGamer2000

Project Description

A collection of self-arranged tracks from various projects compiled into one program, inspired by Sega Music Collection!

Projects include (in acronyms) GHM3, GHM4, NMRTT, SONNM, GTGSGTE, and SONSL.

You must have a Sega Genesis/Mega Drive console or emulator to play this project.

Note that the tracks will sound off-tune and broken on certain emulators (which appears to be a common issue with VGM Play programs).
