History

Team-Art-Forever first started as a Deviantart group and was launched on October 13, 2021 by TheGamer2000. It's origins date back to February 2021 with the unsuccessful launch of TEAM-AFRO. The group gained popularity just 3 days after launch. More admins joined throughout the next 6 months, making the group stronger. The group has recieved a Twitter promotion, a school promotion, and several affiliation requests throughout the months as well. There was a hacker raid back in May 2022, which ended up with the launch of the Security Breach. As of today, August 24, 2022, the group now has off-screen admin, a challenge series, and is on it's way to making it's way to reaching a year in service.
